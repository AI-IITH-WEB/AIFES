Research Themes

Four Interlocking Research Pillars

Each theme stands alone but amplifies the others — Finance informs Economy models; Economy models shape Society impact analysis.

Theme A
Finance
  • Financial Analytics — time-series & factor research, portfolio analytics, nowcasting, alternative-data fusion
  • Algorithmic Trading — strategy discovery, adaptive execution, order-book analytics, market impact
  • Risk Management — market, credit, liquidity & operational risk; ML-assisted early warning; explainable scores
  • AI in Finance — document intelligence, graph-based entity linking, retrieval pipelines
Theme B
Economies
  • FinTech & DeFi — CBDC/DeFi analytics, payments & lending, financial inclusion, KYC/AML
  • Token Economy & Market Design — mechanism & incentive alignment, governance, reputation systems
  • Climate Finance & Energy — transition/physical risk, carbon markets, green bonds, ESG assurance, power-market analytics
Theme C
Society
  • RegTech & SupTech — model-risk management, compliance automation, audit trails, standards mapping
  • Computational Game Theory — strategic behaviour, auctions, credit-rating games, platform policies
  • AI's Impact on Economy & Society — productivity & jobs, fairness & safety, alignment, policy simulation
Theme D
Mathematical Finance
  • Stochastic Calculus — Brownian motion, Ito processes, stochastic differential equations
  • Derivative Pricing — option pricing theory, Black–Scholes models, arbitrage-free pricing
  • Financial Mathematics — martingale methods, risk-neutral measures, portfolio optimization
  • Quantitative Methods — mathematical modeling of financial markets and economic systems

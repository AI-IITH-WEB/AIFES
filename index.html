About the Lab

A New Kind of Finance Research Lab

Where rigorous academic research meets deployable applied technology.

AIFES is a research laboratory at IIT Hyderabad dedicated to advancing trustworthy AI across the full spectrum of finance, economies, and society.

We fuse market science with data-centric engineering to deliver reproducible methods, open datasets, and deployable tools — not just papers.

Our work spans from algorithmic trading and order-book analytics to climate risk, central bank digital currencies, RegTech compliance, and the societal impact of AI on labour markets and fairness.

We embed responsible AI — evaluation, assurance, and transparency — throughout every project, not as an afterthought.

Objective 01
Rigorous Research
Peer-reviewed publications, policy briefs, and industry reports grounded in sound empirical methodology.
Objective 02
Applied Prototypes
Ship working tools with industry partners — forecasting engines, execution algorithms, anomaly detectors, and controls-as-code.
Objective 03
Talent Development
Build the next generation of AI-fluent finance professionals via projects, micro-courses, and executive education.
Objective 04
Responsible AI
Evaluation, assurance, and transparency embedded throughout — not bolted on. Every output ships with explainability and audit trails.

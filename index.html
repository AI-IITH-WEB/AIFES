About the Lab

A New Kind of
Finance Research Lab

Where rigorous academic research meets deployable applied technology.

AIFES is a research laboratory at IIT Hyderabad dedicated to advancing trustworthy AI across the full spectrum of finance, economies, and society.

We fuse market science with data-centric engineering to deliver reproducible methods, open datasets, and deployable tools — not just papers.

Our work spans from algorithmic trading and order-book analytics to climate risk, central bank digital currencies, RegTech compliance, and the societal impact of AI on labour markets and fairness.

We embed responsible AI — evaluation, assurance, and transparency — throughout every project, not as an afterthought.

Objective 01
Rigorous Research
Peer-reviewed publications, policy briefs, and industry reports grounded in sound empirical methodology.
Objective 02
Applied Prototypes
Ship working tools with industry partners — forecasting engines, execution algorithms, anomaly detectors, and controls-as-code.
Objective 03
Talent Development
Build the next generation of AI-fluent finance professionals via projects, micro-courses, and executive education.
Objective 04
Responsible AI
Evaluation, assurance, and transparency embedded throughout — not bolted on. Every output ships with explainability and audit trails.

Research Themes

Four Interlocking
Research Pillars

Each theme stands alone but amplifies the others — Finance informs Economy models; Economy models shape Society impact analysis.

📈
Theme A
Finance
  • Financial Analytics — time-series & factor research, portfolio analytics, nowcasting, alternative-data fusion
  • Algorithmic Trading — strategy discovery, adaptive execution, order-book analytics, market impact
  • Risk Management — market, credit, liquidity & operational risk; ML-assisted early warning; explainable scores
  • AI in Finance — document intelligence, graph-based entity linking, retrieval pipelines
🌐
Theme B
Economies
  • FinTech & DeFi — CBDC/DeFi analytics, payments & lending, financial inclusion, KYC/AML
  • Token Economy & Market Design — mechanism & incentive alignment, governance, reputation systems
  • Climate Finance & Energy — transition/physical risk, carbon markets, green bonds, ESG assurance, power-market analytics
⚖️
Theme C
Society
  • RegTech & SupTech — model-risk management, compliance automation, audit trails, standards mapping
  • Computational Game Theory — strategic behaviour, auctions, credit-rating games, platform policies
  • AI's Impact on Economy & Society — productivity & jobs, fairness & safety, alignment, policy simulation
∑
Theme D
Mathematical Finance
  • Stochastic Calculus — Brownian motion, Ito processes, stochastic differential equations
  • Derivative Pricing — option pricing theory, Black–Scholes models, arbitrage-free pricing
  • Financial Mathematics — martingale methods, risk-neutral measures, portfolio optimization
  • Quantitative Methods — mathematical modeling of financial markets and economic systems

Research Community

AIFES Research Reading Group

A collaborative forum where faculty and students explore the mathematical foundations and research directions in AI for Finance, Economies, and Society.

The AIFES Reading Group brings together faculty members and students interested in research at the intersection of artificial intelligence, financial markets, economic systems, and societal impact.

Faculty — IIT Hyderabad
Prof. Ganesh Ghalme
Faculty — IIT Hyderabad
Prof. V L Raju Chinthalapati

The group meets regularly and is structured around presentations delivered by a designated presenter for each session. These sessions provide an opportunity to study foundational concepts, discuss important ideas in quantitative finance and economic systems, and develop a deeper understanding of the mathematical and computational tools required for research in this area.

The discussions focus on core topics such as probability theory, stochastic processes, and stochastic calculus, along with their applications in financial modeling and market analysis.

Through collaborative discussions and presentations, the reading group aims to cultivate a strong research culture and prepare participants to engage with contemporary research problems and contribute to the broader research agenda in AI for Finance, Economies, and Society.

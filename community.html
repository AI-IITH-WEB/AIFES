Community & Outreach

AIFES Dialogues & Open Tech Days

Monthly events, weekly reading groups, and semester hack nights — open to students, researchers, and practitioners.

AIFES Dialogues
Monthly talks spanning Finance, Economies, and Society. All sessions recorded with slides and code artefacts published openly.
Open Tech Days
Live demos of toolkits and student/startup projects. Media and partner engagement. Hands-on workshops with real data and tools.
Research Presentations
Monthly research presentations, weekly reading groups, and cross-theme hack nights every semester.
Quant & Algo Club
Student club focused on quantitative finance, algorithmic trading, and strategy development with real market data.
DeFi / Token Club
Exploring decentralised finance, token mechanism design, and on-chain analytics with student-led research projects.
Startup Support
Office hours, pitch prep, data and compute credits, and compliance guidance via the IITH incubation cell.
